**Appearance**

Valerya is a weathered woman with sharp eyes, dark hair streaked with silver, and hands roughened by years of work. She wears a faded wool dress beneath a patched leather apron filled with small pouches and tools. Her stall is always tidy, but she constantly rearranges items—part habit, part nervous energy.

She rarely smiles at strangers, not because she is cruel, but because she has learned that everyone who approaches her usually wants something.

**Demeanor**

Valerya speaks in short, careful sentences. She watches people before answering them, weighing whether they are a customer, a threat, or someone bringing trouble. She avoids sharing personal details and changes the subject whenever conversation turns toward her family or the past.

Her stress shows in small ways: tapping fingers, checking the road behind customers, counting coins twice, and apologizing when there is no reason to.

**Background**

Valerya has run her market stall in [[./Dunland|Dunland]] for nearly twenty years. She knows every villager’s habits, debts, and secrets, though she pretends not to. Recently, business has become harder. Deliveries have been delayed, prices have risen, and strange travelers have begun asking questions about the village.

Valerya suspects something is wrong but fears that speaking up will draw attention to herself.

  
**Mannerisms**  

- Keeps one hand near her coin pouch or a hidden knife.
- Cleans her stall whenever she is anxious.
- Remembers everyone’s purchases but pretends she doesn’t.
- Gives extra food to struggling villagers while claiming it was “going bad anyway.”
- Looks toward the village road whenever she hears hoofbeats.

Valerya is not a warrior, but she is not helpless:

- Weapon: Small utility knife hidden in her apron
- Strengths: Quick thinking, street knowledge, knowing the village layout
- Weakness: Panic under pressure; she freezes when situations become chaotic
