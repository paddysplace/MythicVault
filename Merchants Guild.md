**The Gilded Vein Consortium**

![[./Media/Organisations/Merchants Guild.png|697]]

**Type:** Merchant Guild (Publicly Legitimate, Privately Corrupt)  
**Leader:** Valen Drax, _“Trade Diplomat”_ (secret Guildmaster)  
**Motto:** _“From Stone, Wealth. From Wealth, Power.”_

---

### **Overview**

The Gilded Vein began as a collective of traders managing ore exports and blacksmith supply chains. Over time, it grew into a shadowy economic force that quietly controls most of the city’s wealth.

Officially, it regulates trade.  
In truth, it _owns it_.

Most citizens never question it—because prices stay stable, goods keep flowing… and those who dig too deep disappear.

---

### **Structure**

#### **Valen Drax — The Silent Master**

- Operates as guildmaster behind his diplomatic role
- Never publicly linked to the Consortium
- Uses contracts filled with hidden clauses to bind members

#### **The Inner Circle (“The Coin Court”)**

A small group of elite merchants and financiers:

- Control mining rights
- Set market prices
- Approve or sabotage major trade deals

Each member owes their wealth—and secrets—to Valen.

---

#### **Guild Roles**

- **Ledgermen:** Accountants who manipulate records and taxes
- **Chain Brokers:** Enforcers disguised as traders; handle “problems”
- **Market Voices:** Spies in stalls, taverns, and stables
- **Ore Factors:** Oversee mining operations—and skim profits

---

### **Operations & Corruption**

#### 💰 **Monopoly Control**

- Artificially raise or lower metal prices
- Sabotage independent blacksmiths who refuse guild contracts
- Control supply routes through intimidation

#### ⚒️ **Mining Exploitation**

- Force smaller miners into unfair agreements
- Hide dangerous or illegal excavation sites
- Secretly fund deeper digs near the archaeological site

---

#### ⚖️ **Political Influence**

- Bribe officials in the Mayor’s Office
- Influence school teachings to favor guild loyalty
- Use the church to legitimize “just trade practices”

---

#### ⚔️ **Illegal Activities**

- Smuggling rare metals and forbidden artifacts
- Quietly running extortion rings disguised as “insurance”
- Silencing rivals through staged accidents or disappearances

---

### **Key Locations**

- **The Marble Exchange Hall:** Guild headquarters, clean and respectable on the surface
- **Hidden Vault Beneath the Market:** Stores illicit goods and sensitive records
- **The Broken Ledger Room:** A secret archive of debts, blackmail, and contracts
- **Tavern Backrooms:** Used for quiet negotiations and threats

---

### **Relationship with the City**

- **Blacksmiths:** Many resent them, but depend on them
- **Miners:** Exploited but fearful of speaking out
- **Citizens:** Unaware of the full extent of control
- **Garrick Ironhand:** Actively distrusts them and refuses involvement

---

### **Rumors & Story Hooks**

- The Consortium is seeking something hidden beneath the archaeological site
- A ledger exists that could expose the entire organization
- Valen Drax has a fallback plan to collapse the city’s economy—and rebuild it under total control
- Someone inside the Coin Court wants Valen removed… but fears what would replace him
