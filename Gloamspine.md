- **Build**: Towering at 15–20 feet at the shoulder, with a heavily muscled, broad-chested frame. Limbs are thick and clawed, built for both immense strength and bursts of speed despite its massive size.

- **Fur & Skin:** Thick fur interspersed with patches of armored, bark-like skin. The fur is a dark midnight brown with streaks of iridescent silver or crimson that shimmer in moonlight.

- **Head & Features:** Large, imposing head with a wide snout, an array of jagged teeth visible even when the mouth is closed. Eyes glow faintly amber or icy blue, reflecting intelligence and ferocity. Horn-like bony ridges curve along the skull, giving it a demi-demonic appearance.

- **Limbs & Claws:** Forelimbs end in enormous, retractable claws capable of rending stone. Paws are heavily padded but with talon-like protrusions. Hind limbs are slightly more digitigrade for leaping prowess.

- **Tail & Extra Features**: A thick, muscular tail tipped with a spiked club-like formation. Occasional faint bioluminescent patterns run along its back and shoulders, pulsing with its heartbeat or emotional state.

- **Aura & Environment:** Often depicted in misty, enchanted forests or volcanic terrain, accentuating its mythical and territorial nature. Its physical presence is terrifying yet majestic, radiating primal power.
