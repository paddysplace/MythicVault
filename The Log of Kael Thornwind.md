### S1 Travel through the forest



? Do we see any creatures in the forest nearby?

=> The Gloamspine is too preoccupied with eating to even notice us and moves away from our direction.

We move further into the forest until we come to a glade with young trees growing.

? Do we find anything interesting in the glade?
-> No
=> Nothing we want to be near. In the center sits an ancient being. It emits a feeling of fear, dread. It is pale, it doesn't even appear to be alive. 

? Does it wake up as we try to make our way around it?
-> Yes
=> The being sees us move and transforms into a large albino wolf. All teeth and claws and not looking at all friendly at our intrusion. It lunges at us in a full on attack. There is no way we can outrun it. We must stand our ground. 

Large Wolf 
			AC: 6
			Hit Dice: 4
			Attack: Bite

Special: On a miss it will heal one wound.

Round 1

I swing as the beast charges at us but it's too quick and dodges out the way. It leaps over both of us and turns to attack. Setting its sights on Valerya. It slashes wildly but Valerya is too quick and the beast misses.

Round 2

I charge at the beast before it recovers from missing Valerya and plunge my sword into its hind quarters. Causing a vicious wound. Valerya moves in whilst I keep the wolf occupied. The small knife is not enough to cause a wound. And to our horror the wolf appears to be slowly healing itself. The beast snarls and lunges at Valerya again. Seeing her blade as no danger to itself. Its claw causing a minor wound. 

Round 3

We need to end this quickly. My sword slashes wildly and slides off the wolfs sleek coat. Valerya lunges with her knife. But fails to even make contact. The wolf growls triumphantly. It deep cut in its flank closes up completely. I roll out the way and the wolf growls in frustration.

Round 4

I slash in equal frustration cutting the beast deeply again. Valerya again attempts to distract the wolf at least allowing me to get another attack in. Again failing to penetrate the dense fur and skin. We avoid its attack.

Round 5

The beast still wounded I hope to end this fight quickly. I cause a small tear in the wolfs face. We are slowly wearing it down. Valerya follows up with a similar wound, temporarily blinding the creature as blood pours down its face. Turning its anger at Valerya once again it goes in for the kill. Stumbling, the wolf crashes down hard on the ground.

Round 6

Our moment could have arrived. With the wolf on the ground I plunge my sword into its neck severing an artery. Before our eyes the body of the wolf crumbles into dust and disappears. Behind us we hear a deep whine. Turning we see the ancient being. Back in its original form. We make haste to run back into the depths of the forest once again.
