A small town of roughly 1000 people, Dunland, in the Kingdom of [[./Kingdom of Thaloria|Kingdom of Thaloria]] is known mainly for its pottery. Although it's tall towers are colourful the town has fallen into hard times, leaving it unsafe and corrupt. The local people are welcoming and kind to newcomers, which is at odd with the ruling thieves guild.
